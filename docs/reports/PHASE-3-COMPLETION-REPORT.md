## 🌈 七轴系统架构

### 📊 组合数学验证

```
七轴定义：
├── 模式轴 (Mode): 3种配置 (light, dark, auto)
├── 色调轴 (Hue): 7种配置 (blue, green, purple, orange, red, teal, pink)
├── 饱和度轴 (Saturation): 4种配置 (muted, normal, vibrant, monochrome)
├── 亮度轴 (Lightness): 3种配置 (bright, normal, dim)
├── 密度轴 (Density): 3种配置 (compact, comfortable, spacious)
├── 圆度轴 (Roundness): 3种配置 (sharp, rounded, circular)
└── 对比度轴 (Contrast): 4种配置 (subtle, standard, strong, extreme)

总组合数 = 3 × 7 × 4 × 3 × 3 × 3 × 4 = **5,103种**
```
